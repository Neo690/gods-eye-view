- The app updates panel now reserves its most prominent slot for the platform the console actually runs on: a dedicated amber button - "Get the Windows update" on a desktop, "Get the Android update" on a phone - sits above the per-channel rows whenever a newer build is published on that platform's channel, and opens the artefact straight from the panel. When the console is already current, the same slot dims to a calmer "open release" button instead of pretending an update is waiting, and a channel that has never been published (iOS without signing, for now) gets no hero at all - its row keeps saying so. Translation of the new label is complete across the five app languages. (yourname, #1331)
